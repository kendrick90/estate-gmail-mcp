Privacy Policy: Estate Gmail MCP

Estate Gmail MCP is a personal tool used only by its owner on his own computer.

  • It accesses Gmail data only for accounts the owner signs in to, and only to read, search and draft email for his own use.
  • Sign-in tokens and any data it retrieves stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
